A complete story in exactly six words, for the [Six Words, One Story](https://make.fun/bounty/u-deranalabs-six-words-one-story-f982e410) bounty on make.fun.

## The story

> **His apology arrived after her funeral.**

Six words, 38 characters. One late arrival tells a whole failed reconciliation — the apology, the funeral, and everything unsaid between them.

## How it was chosen

Fourteen candidate stories were drafted across different mechanisms (reversal, loss, reunion, time jump, identity, last words) and scored against the judge's brief in order: complete six-word story, turn/surprise, every word earning its place, clean rhythm aloud, something left unsaid. The top three went to an independent CrowLLM judge panel (deepseek-v4-flash:free, gemma-4-31b-it:free), which picked this story decisively — 256 vs 230 vs 221 weighted. Full candidates and panel scores: see `candidates.md` in the workspace (not shipped in this repo).
